Why Changing Your Actions Hasn't Worked - The TEAR Model

This framework explains why your efforts to manage time and priorities haven't resolved the underlying tension:

Thoughts → Emotion → Action → Results

Your thoughts create emotion (energy in motion). That emotion provides the energy to take action, which leads to your results.

When most people try to change their results, they focus on changing their actions. But you can't sustain new actions without addressing the thoughts and emotions driving your current behavior.

You already know what you should do—delegate more, set boundaries, prioritize ruthlessly. But you don't do it. The reason? You don't feel like it.

That feeling—that emotion—is the step most people skip. They jump straight from knowing what to do to trying to force the action, completely bypassing the energy required to sustain it.

So you try to force yourself. You create elaborate systems, set strict schedules, commit to saying no. And it works—for a while. But rarely consistently. Why? Forcing action without shifting the underlying thought pattern leaves you split: your body is in one place while your head and your heart feel like they're in another. Ever felt that disconnect?
